# Load the panel on demand and keep it

Each bar widget used to create its `Panel.qml` when the shell started, through a `Loader` that was always active, so a shell with three monitors held three panels that were closed most of the day. Measured offscreen with the service over a copy of a real database, 20 s after the start, three readings each, a shell without the plugin used 116.9 MB of RSS (23.6 MB anonymous), and with the plugin on three monitors 175.9 MB (71.8 MB anonymous). With no panel created the same shell used 130.7 MB, so the three panels cost about 45 MB. On one monitor the plugin used 153.6 MB, 23 MB over the shell with no panel, so the first panel with the compile of `Panel.qml` costs about 23 MB and each further panel about 11 MB.

`BarWidget.qml` now creates its panel the first time it is wanted, and keeps it until the shell reloads:

- The pointer entering the bar icon (`WidgetButton.tooltipHovered`) calls `preparePanel()`, which starts the `Loader` with `asynchronous: true`, so the hover does not stall the bar.
- `open()` loads the panel synchronously when it is not there yet, then opens it. Every path that shows the panel goes through it: a click (`togglePanel`), the `scratchpad` IPC (`open`, `show`, `toggle`), `shell.summon` and `Bar.switchPanelFrom`, which call `open()` on the widget that `Bar.findPanelWidget` picked, and a click forwarded from another panel's overlay. While another plugin's panel is open, its `KeyboardPanel` overlay covers the screen, so the Omanotes button never sees the pointer. The click reaches it through `KeyboardPanel.forwardBarClick` and `WidgetButton.triggerPress`, with no hover before it, and the load is synchronous on that path every time. The ring card never opens the panel.
- When a hover already started a load in the background, `open()` turns `asynchronous` off, and the `Loader` finishes that load at once, so a click during the load opens the panel in the same call.
- `preparePanel()` hands the panel `bar`, `settings`, `anchorItem`, `hostWidget`, `db` and `service` as initial properties of `Loader.setSource`, so the panel has the widget's Db before any of its objects completes. The Db has usually loaded long before the panel, and no `itemsUpdated` or `historyChanged` comes for the rows it already holds. So the Items tab and the History tab select their first row in their own `Component.onCompleted`, as they did on the first load when the panel was created at the start. The panel shows its rows in the turn it is created, and the hover sends no request to the database. `injectPanel()` still passes on the values that change later.
- The widget keeps what the host needs: `open`, `close` and `opened` for `Bar.findPanelWidget`, `popoutSwitchClosing` and `closeForPopoutSwitch` for `Bar.requestPopout`, and `openPanelIndicatorWidth`. `opened` is false while no panel is loaded, and `close()` does nothing then. `panelItem` is the `Loader`'s item, so it is null until the first load.
- A `Panel.qml` that fails to load leaves the `Loader` in `Loader.Error`. The widget logs one line, `open()` then does nothing, and nothing throws. The widget does not load again: the engine keeps the failed compile for that URL until the shell restarts. The `Loader` loads once per widget, so `asynchronous` is not set back after the first load.

## Considered options

- Keep the panels loaded. It costs about 45 MB on three monitors for a popout that is closed most of the time.
- Load on the first open only, synchronously. It adds the whole load to every first click, where the hover hides most of it.
- Load on demand and unload the panel after a minute closed and idle. It was built and reviewed, and refused. Measured offscreen on one monitor without the service, the shell used 148 876 kB with the panel loaded and 148 808 kB after the unload, so the unload gave back 68 kB. Sixty cycles of hover and unload gave back nothing either. The freed memory stays with the process for the next load, and the unload adds nothing to the saving. It cost a rule for each thing that only a closed panel holds (an untitled draft, a write that failed, a write in flight, the sound file chooser, the panel's own `Updater`), and a panel loaded again lost its selected row, its scroll position and its Settings page.
- Compile `Panel.qml` at the start without creating it, and create the panel on demand. It would keep the guarantee of ADR-0017 below and take the compile out of the first open, for about 12 MB held from the start (inferred: the first panel costs about 23 MB with the compile and each further one about 11 MB). Refused. The gap it closes lasts from the merge of an update to the restart, and the update restarts the shell by itself.

## Consequences

- Measured offscreen with the service over a copy of the same database, 20 s after the start, three readings each. On three monitors the shell used 130.7 MB of RSS and 30.5 MB anonymous, against 175.9 MB and 71.8 MB with the panels created at the start. On one monitor it used 130.3 MB and 30.0 MB, against 153.6 MB and 49.5 MB. The plugin costs about 14 MB of RSS over the shell whatever the number of monitors, until a panel is wanted. With every panel opened and closed once, three monitors used 171.1 MB, against 179.4 MB with the panels created at the start and used the same way. Most of the saving is for the monitors whose panel is never opened.
- The load moves to the first hover or open of each panel. Measured offscreen, three shells of each case, the first load of a shell cold (it also compiles `Panel.qml`) and five more warm, each on a new bar widget. A hover loads the panel in the background in 28 to 38 ms warm and 62 to 66 ms cold, before a click can come. An open with no hover, from the IPC, a hotkey or a click forwarded by `triggerPress`, takes 29 to 36 ms warm and 53 to 55 ms cold, where it took 0 to 2 ms with the panel loaded at the start. Every later open of the same panel costs what it cost before.
- The numbers above were taken with the kit's `KeyboardPanel` replaced by a plain `FloatingWindow` (`stub_keyboard_panel` in `test/lib/harness.sh`), since the real one is a layer-shell `PanelWindow` with no backend offscreen. They leave out the creation of the real window, its `TransformWatcher`, its `BorderSurface` and its dismiss windows, on the first open of each panel. No reading was taken on the live shell.
- ADR-0017 relies on the shell keeping the components it compiled at the start, so an update that merged new files runs the old code until the restart. A panel that was never hovered or opened in this session is now compiled from disk on its first hover or open. Between the merge of an update and the restart, or after a restart that failed (`restartFailed`), such a panel runs the new `Panel.qml` and `ui/` with the old `BarWidget`, `Store`, `ItemsDb` and `Db.js` (inferred from the compile on demand; no update was run to show it). A `Panel.qml` that does not load fails on its first hover or open, where it used to fail at the start.
- A panel lives from its first hover or open to the next reload of the shell. It keeps its selected row, its scroll position, the Settings page shown, and anything unsaved, across every close, as before this ADR.
- In a shell without the service, the panel's own `Updater` is created with the panel, and runs `update.sh status` then, not at the start.
- Offscreen, a widget at the top left corner of its window is under the pointer from the start, so it loads its panel. `test/panel.sh` and `test/startup.sh` place the widget away from that corner. `test/panel.sh` drives the hover and the click with `QtTest`'s `TestEvent`.

## Gates

Each gate failed with its defect planted and passes without it.

- No panel exists after the start: `test/startup.sh` with three widgets and the service, and `test/panel.sh`, where the list IPC and a `close` load none. They failed with `preparePanel()` called when the `Loader` completes.
- An IPC `open` with no hover loads the panel and opens it at once, and a click in the same turn as the hover, before the background load ends, opens it with its rows (`test/panel.sh`). They failed with `open()` leaving `asynchronous` on.
- A panel created after its Db loaded shows every row, the first one selected and in the editor, and the newest history entry selected, in the turn it is created, on the synchronous and on the background path, and the hover sends no request (`test/panel.sh`, which counts the lines of `db.log`). They failed with `db` injected after the load instead of given to `setSource`, with the Items or History tab not selecting in `Component.onCompleted`, and with the Items tab asking the Db to reload when created.
- A loaded panel uses its widget's Db and adds no Db, `SystemClock` or `RingWindow` of its own (`test/startup.sh`, after every widget loads its panel). It failed with an `ItemsDb` or a `SystemClock` added to `Panel.qml`, and with the widget handing the panel no Db.
- With a `Panel.qml` that does not compile, the widget still answers the IPC, an `open`, a hover and a `toggle` open nothing, and the log holds one line for the failure and no `TypeError` (`test/panel.sh`, in a second shell). It failed with `open()` calling the missing panel, and with the log line removed. A `preparePanel()` that calls `setSource` again after the failure passed it too, since the `Loader` stays in `Loader.Error`, so the gate does not tell the two apart; no retry is attempted because the engine keeps the failed compile until a restart.
- `test/render.sh` draws the same pictures as before. Against a run of the old code, 13 to 15 of 20 shots are the same bytes, and each other shot differs as much between two runs of the old code (History, Trash and the five Settings pages).
